Health Services

Promoting student health, safety, and well-being within the school community.

School Clinic

The school clinic provides basic first aid, health assessment, and immediate care for students and staff during school hours.

Medical Screening

Regular health checks and screenings are conducted to monitor student wellness and identify health concerns early.

Emergency Response

Established procedures are in place to respond to medical emergencies and ensure timely referral to appropriate health facilities.

Health Education

Health-related information and awareness activities are provided to support healthy habits and informed decision-making.

Sanitation & Hygiene

The school maintains sanitation and hygiene standards to support a clean, safe, and healthy learning environment.

Coordination & Referral

The school coordinates with parents, local health units, and partners when further medical attention is required.
