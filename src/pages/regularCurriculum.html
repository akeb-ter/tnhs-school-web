Regular Junior High School Curriculum

The Regular Curriculum provides a balanced and comprehensive Junior High School education that develops academic competence, character, and readiness for Senior High School pathways.

Program Overview

The Regular Junior High School Curriculum follows the Department of Education’s K to 12 framework, ensuring that learners acquire essential knowledge, skills, and values across core learning areas.

Learning Areas

  • English
  • Filipino
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Araling Panlipunan
  • Technology and Livelihood Education
  • MAPEH
  • Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao

Curriculum Goals

The program aims to develop independent learners who are critical thinkers, effective communicators, and responsible members of the community, prepared for various Senior High School tracks and strands.
