Senior High School TVL Track

The Technical-Vocational-Livelihood Track equips learners with practical skills and industry-based competencies for immediate employment or further specialization.

Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

The ICT strand develops learners’ skills in computer systems, networking, and software applications aligned with industry standards.

Strand Focus

Training in computer systems servicing, networking, troubleshooting, and digital productivity tools.

Possible Career Paths

  • Computer Technician
  • IT Support Staff
  • Network Assistant
  • Technical Support Specialist

Industry Readiness

Graduates are prepared for TESDA certification and entry-level IT-related employment.

Home Economics (HE)

The Home Economics strand focuses on livelihood skills related to food, hospitality, personal services, and entrepreneurship.

Strand Focus

Skills training in cookery, baking, caregiving, and other service-oriented livelihood competencies.

Possible Career Paths

  • Food Service Worker
  • Baker
  • Caregiver
  • Small Business Owner

Industry Readiness

Prepares learners for TESDA certifications and employment in service and hospitality sectors.

Electrical Installation and Maintenance (EIM)

The EIM strand develops technical skills in electrical installation, maintenance, and safety practices.

Strand Focus

Hands-on training in electrical wiring, installation, troubleshooting, and preventive maintenance.

Possible Career Paths

  • Electrician
  • Maintenance Technician
  • Electrical Installer
  • Industrial Worker

Industry Readiness

Equips learners for TESDA certification and employment in electrical and industrial sectors.

Automotive Servicing

The Automotive Servicing strand equips learners with essential skills in vehicle maintenance, diagnostics, and basic repair aligned with industry and TESDA standards.

Strand Focus

Training in automotive preventive maintenance, engine inspection, basic troubleshooting, and workplace safety.

Possible Career Paths

  • Automotive Mechanic
  • Service Technician
  • Auto Maintenance Assistant
  • Garage Technician

Industry Readiness

Prepares learners for TESDA Automotive Servicing NC II certification and entry-level employment in automotive service centers.
