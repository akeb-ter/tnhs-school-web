Senior High School Academic Track

The Academic Track prepares learners for higher education through specialized strands that develop critical thinking, academic excellence, and discipline-specific skills.

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM)

The STEM strand is designed for learners who excel in science and mathematics and aspire to pursue careers in engineering, medicine, information technology, and scientific research.

Strand Focus

Emphasis on advanced mathematics, science, research, and problem-solving skills to prepare learners for STEM-related degree programs.

Possible Career Paths

  • Engineer
  • Doctor
  • Scientist
  • IT Professional

College Readiness

Graduates are well-prepared for university programs in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics fields.

Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM)

The ABM strand equips learners with foundational knowledge in accounting, finance, marketing, and entrepreneurship.

Strand Focus

Focus on business concepts, financial literacy, and management principles.

Possible Career Paths

  • Accountant
  • Entrepreneur
  • Business Manager
  • Marketing Specialist

College Readiness

Prepares learners for business-related college programs and entrepreneurial ventures.

Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS)

The HUMSS strand develops learners’ communication skills, social awareness, and critical thinking in preparation for careers in education, law, and social sciences.

Strand Focus

Emphasis on communication, social studies, philosophy, and cultural understanding.

Possible Career Paths

  • Teacher
  • Lawyer
  • Journalist
  • Social Worker

College Readiness

Prepares learners for humanities and social science degree programs.
