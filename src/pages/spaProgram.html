Special Program in the Arts

A Junior High School program that nurtures creativity, artistic excellence, and cultural awareness through focused training in the arts.

Program Overview

The Special Program in the Arts (SPA) is designed for learners with exceptional talent and interest in the arts. The program provides structured and enriched instruction to develop artistic skills and creative expression.

Program Objectives

  • Develop artistic skills and creative discipline
  • Promote cultural awareness and appreciation
  • Enhance self-expression and confidence
  • Prepare learners for arts-related academic paths

Learning Focus

SPA learners engage in performance-based activities, workshops, and exhibitions that strengthen their foundation in visual arts, music, dance, theater, and other artistic disciplines.
